Each pose must communicate its emotion through silhouette and face, legible at small size. Keep each mascot sufficiently distinct in proportions and personality.

Tiny expressive eyes set on either side of its nose, funny eyebrow movements. The character feels like a laid-back curious daydreamer suddenly becoming overenthusiastic.
